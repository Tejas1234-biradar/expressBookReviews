Task 9 — Add or update a book review

Command:
curl -sS -X PUT http://localhost:5000/customer/auth/review/1 -H 'Authorization: Bearer <TOKEN_FROM_LOGIN>' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"review":"A memorable and thought-provoking book."}'

Output:
{"message":"Review added or updated successfully.","reviews":{"assignment_student_2026":"A memorable and thought-provoking book."}}
